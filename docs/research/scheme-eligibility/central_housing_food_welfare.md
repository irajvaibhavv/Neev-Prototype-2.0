# Central housing, food, farmer, women/child, livelihood, education & construction-worker schemes — eligibility rules (researched 30 Sep 2026)

Conventions: confidence tags [H]/[M]/[L] per criterion. "Official" = govt portal/PIB/Act; "secondary" = bank/news/aggregator. Where only secondary sources were found, that is stated. Items from background knowledge that could not be verified this session are kept in Inferences/Gaps and labelled, never in Cited Findings.

## PM Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

### Takeaway
Family (husband, wife, unmarried children) with annual household income ≤ ₹9 lakh, living in an urban area, owning no pucca house anywhere in India, and not benefited from any govt housing scheme in last 20 years. House must be in female head's name or joint name. Four verticals: BLC, AHP, ARH, ISS.

### Cited Findings
- Income slabs (annual household income): EWS ≤ ₹3 lakh; LIG ₹3–6 lakh; MIG ₹6–9 lakh [H] — [PMAY-U 2.0 FAQ, pmaymis.gov.in](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- NOTE: pmay-urban.gov.in/faq still shows the OLD PMAY-U 1.0 slabs (MIG-I ₹6–12 lakh, MIG-II ₹12–18 lakh, CLSS up to ₹2.67 lakh) — these are superseded; do not use — [pmay-urban.gov.in FAQ (1.0 content)](https://pmay-urban.gov.in/faq)
- Beneficiary family = husband, wife and unmarried children [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Must not own a pucca (all-weather, permanent roof & walls) house in own name or any family member's name anywhere in India [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Pucca house defined as all-weather dwelling with roof and walls of burnt bricks, stone with cement/lime, cement concrete, timber, GI sheets, asbestos sheets, machine-made tiles etc. [H] — [IDBI PMAY-U 2.0 eligibility PDF](https://www.idbi.bank.in/pdf/Eligibility-Criteria-Scheme-PMAY-U-2.pdf)
- House must be in the name of the female head of household or joint name; exceptions where no adult female member (widower, unmarried, transgender) [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Ineligible: anyone who has availed any Central/State/local-body housing scheme in the last 20 years; beneficiaries whose PMAY-U houses were curtailed after 31.12.2023 [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- BLC & AHP assistance: other States Central ₹1.5 lakh + State min ₹1 lakh per unit; NE/Hill states/J&K/Delhi/Puducherry Central ₹2.25 lakh + State min ₹0.25 lakh [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- BLC requires the beneficiary to own vacant land; landless may get heritable, non-transferable land rights from states [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- ISS (Interest Subsidy Scheme): max subsidy ₹1.80 lakh; loan up to ₹25 lakh; house value up to ₹35 lakh; carpet area up to 120 sqm [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx); [Indian Bank ISS page](https://indianbank.bank.in/en/interest-subsidy-scheme-issunder-pradhan-mantri-awas-yojana-urban-2.0)
- New pucca house under 2.0: at least 2 rooms, kitchen, toilet/bathroom, electricity and water [M, secondary summary] — [search summary of pmaymis FAQ / bank pages](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Special focus groups (priority, not extra eligibility): widows, PwD, transgender, SC/ST, minorities, Safai Karmi, street vendors (PM SVANidhi), construction workers, slum dwellers [H] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Documents: Aadhaar, bank details, income certificate/self-declaration, land ownership proof (BLC), undertaking of eligibility [M] — [pmaymis.gov.in FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/PmayFAQ.aspx)
- Only one vertical/component can be availed [H] — [pmay-urban.gov.in FAQ](https://pmay-urban.gov.in/faq)

### Inferences
- Questionnaire must-ask: urban resident? annual family income? owns pucca house (any family member, anywhere)? availed govt housing in last 20 yrs? adult woman in family? For zero false positives, show "likely eligible" only if income ≤ ₹9 lakh AND no pucca house AND no prior housing benefit. BLC/AHP subsidy is EWS-only (≤ ₹3 lakh); ISS for EWS/LIG/MIG.
- ARH (Affordable Rental Housing) targets urban migrants/workers/industrial labour — relevant for Neev's user base but no individual subsidy.

### Gaps
- ARH-specific eligibility (rent terms, who qualifies) not extracted from official source.
- States may modify income criteria with ministry approval (stated for 1.0; unclear for 2.0).

## PM Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G), 2024-29 phase

### Takeaway
Rural houseless / kutcha-house families identified via SECC 2011 + Awaas+ survey, verified by Gram Sabha; not self-apply in the usual sense. Revised (Sep 2024) exclusions: income > ₹15,000/month, motorised 3/4-wheeler, mechanised 3/4-wheel agri equipment, KCC ≥ ₹50,000, govt employee, registered non-agri enterprise, income-tax/professional-tax payer, irrigated land thresholds. Two-wheeler, fridge, landline, fishing boat no longer exclude.

### Cited Findings
- Monthly income exclusion threshold raised to ₹15,000 (from ₹10,000); two-wheeler, motorised fishing boat, refrigerator, landline removed as exclusions; exclusion criteria reduced from 13 to 10 [M, secondary] — [Business Today, 11 Sep 2024](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/pmay-g-automatic-exclusion-norms-relaxed-details-on-centres-rural-housing-scheme-here-445380-2024-09-11); [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/centre-eases-exclusion-criteria-for-pmay-gramin-new-eligibility-norms-explained/)
- Remaining exclusions: motorised 3/4-wheeler; mechanised 3/4-wheel agricultural equipment; KCC limit ≥ ₹50,000; any member a govt employee; non-agri enterprise registered with govt; income-tax/professional-tax payers; 2.5 acres+ irrigated land (with irrigation equipment) [M, secondary] — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/pmay-g-automatic-exclusion-norms-relaxed-details-on-centres-rural-housing-scheme-here-445380-2024-09-11); [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/centre-eases-exclusion-criteria-for-pmay-gramin-new-eligibility-norms-explained/)
- Assistance: ₹1.20 lakh plains; ₹1.30 lakh hilly/NE/IAP districts; plus ~90 days MGNREGA wages (~₹18,000) and ₹12,000 toilet (SBM-G) [M] — [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/centre-eases-exclusion-criteria-for-pmay-gramin-new-eligibility-norms-explained/)
- Beneficiaries identified from SECC data and verified by Gram Sabhas [M] — [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/centre-eases-exclusion-criteria-for-pmay-gramin-new-eligibility-norms-explained/)
- ₹54,000 cr+ allocated FY 2024-25 [M] — [News on AIR, 20 Nov 2024](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/over-%e2%82%b954000-cr-allocated-in-this-financial-year-to-implement-pmay-g-ministry-of-rural-development)

### Inferences
- Other land exclusions from the older list (5 acres irrigated for 2+ seasons; 7.5 acres with irrigation equipment) likely still apply — background knowledge, unverified this session [L].
- App logic: rural + houseless/kutcha + none of the exclusions → "may be eligible; check Awaas+ list / Gram Panchayat". Never promise inclusion since selection is survey-based. Neev's salaried users earning >₹15,000/month are excluded.

### Gaps
- Official pmayg.nic.in notification with verbatim 10-item list not retrieved; relied on secondary sources. Awaas+ 2024 survey window/app (Awaas+ 2024 self-survey) not verified.

## PM-KISAN

### Takeaway
Landholding farmer families (land in their name in land records) get income support; excluded: institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, ministers/MPs/MLAs/mayors/zila panchayat chairs, serving/retired govt employees (except MTS/Class IV/Group D), pensioners with ≥ ₹10,000/month, last-AY income-tax payers, registered professionals. Land acquired after 01.02.2019 (except inheritance) generally not eligible.

### Cited Findings
- Pensioners with monthly pension ≥ ₹10,000 excluded (MTS/Class IV/Group D exempt) [M, secondary] — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/savings-schemes/eligibility-for-pm-kisan-samman-nidhi-yojana); [Upstox 2026 guide](https://upstox.com/learning-center/personal-finance/pm-kisan-eligibility-criteria-2026-a-complete-guide/article-1720/)
- Excluded: anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year; registered doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects; institutional landholders; present/former constitutional post holders, ministers, MPs, MLAs/MLCs, mayors, district panchayat chairpersons [M, secondary] — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/savings-schemes/eligibility-for-pm-kisan-samman-nidhi-yojana)
- Land ownership acquired after 01.02.2019 not eligible (except inheritance) [M, secondary] — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/savings-schemes/eligibility-for-pm-kisan-samman-nidhi-yojana)

### Inferences
- Benefit ₹6,000/year in three ₹2,000 instalments via DBT; family = husband, wife, minor children; e-KYC + land seeding + Aadhaar-bank link mandatory; all landholding size limits removed in 2019 — background knowledge, not verified this session [M].
- For Neev: most blue-collar urban migrants won't own land in own name; a family member might. Questionnaire: "Is agricultural land registered in your/spouse's name?" + income-tax payer? + govt employee/pensioner ≥₹10k?

### Gaps
- Official pmkisan.gov.in eligibility page not fetched; confirm benefit amount and any 2025-26 change (e.g. farmer ID/Agristack requirement) there.

## NFSA ration card / PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)

### Takeaway
Two categories: Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY, poorest; 35 kg/household/month) and Priority Household (PHH; 5 kg/person/month). Grain free under PMGKAY since 1 Jan 2023, extended 5 years from 1 Jan 2024. States identify PHH using their own criteria; AAY per central guidelines. Not a self-assessable eligibility — the app should route to state ration-card application.

### Cited Findings
- AAY 35 kg per household/month; PHH 5 kg per person/month [H] — [PIB Year-End Review DFPD 2024](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2086345&reg=48&lang=2)
- Free food grains to AAY and PHH under PMGKAY from 1 Jan 2023 [H] — [PIB Year-End Review 2024](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2086345&reg=48&lang=2)
- PMGKAY extended for five years (from 1 Jan 2024) [M] — [Deccan Chronicle, 29 Nov 2023](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/in-other-news/291123/pradhan-mantri-garib-kalyan-anna-yojana-pmgkay-extended-for-five-yea.html)
- Fortified rice supply under PMGKAY approved July 2024 – Dec 2028 [H] — [PIB Year-End Review 2024](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2086345&reg=48&lang=2)
- PHH identified by states' guidelines; AAY per Union guidelines; coverage ~81 crore people [M] — [Wikipedia: PMGKAY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradhan_Mantri_Garib_Kalyan_Anna_Yojana)

### Inferences
- NFSA covers up to 75% rural / 50% urban population (Act statute) — background knowledge [H but unverified here]. One Nation One Ration Card lets migrant workers draw grain anywhere — highly relevant to Neev users; unverified this session.
- No national income cut-off exists; any income number would be state-specific → app must not decide eligibility, only "you may already have/apply for a ration card in your state".

### Gaps
- State-specific PHH inclusion/exclusion criteria not researched (out of scope: central only).

## PM Ujjwala Yojana 2.0 (PMUY)

### Takeaway
Adult woman (18+) from a poor household, no existing LPG connection from any OMC in the household, eligibility by 14-point deprivation declaration (self-declaration). Migrants can self-declare address.

### Cited Findings
- Applicant must be a woman aged 18+; no other LPG connection from any OMC in the same household; must belong to a poor household via deprivation declaration in prescribed format [H] — [pmuy.gov.in Ujjwala 2.0](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/ujjwala2.html)
- 14-point deprivation declaration; includes exclusion items such as a household member being a govt employee, owning > 2.5 acres irrigated land with 1 irrigation equipment or ≥5 acres irrigated for 2+ seasons, owning a motorised 3/4-wheeler/fishing boat [H] — [PMUY Deprivation Declaration PDF](https://pmuy.gov.in/documents/Deprivation_Declaration_for_Poor_Households_English.pdf)
- Documents: KYC form; Aadhaar of applicant and adult family members; address proof (self-declaration for migrants); ration card or state document of family composition; bank passbook/cheque; deprivation declaration [H] — [pmuy.gov.in Ujjwala 2.0](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/ujjwala2.html)
- SOP for release of additional 75 lakh connections (Oct 2023) [H] — [MoPNG SOP PDF](https://mopng.gov.in/files/marketing/lpg/75L-PMUY-SoP-10102023-(1).pdf)

### Inferences
- Benefits (free deposit-free connection, first refill + stove free under 2.0; targeted subsidy ₹300/cylinder for up to 12 refills/yr) — background knowledge, not verified this session [M]; confirm current subsidy on PIB before displaying.
- Questionnaire: woman 18+ in household? any LPG connection in household? any 14-point exclusion (govt employee, 3/4-wheeler, big irrigated land, etc.)?

### Gaps
- Full text of all 14 deprivation-declaration items not extracted; fetch the PDF for exact list. Whether new connections are still being released in 2026 (75 lakh target) not confirmed.

## Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

### Takeaway
Small savings account for a girl child, opened by parent/guardian from birth until she turns 10; max two accounts per family (third allowed for twins/triplets). No income/caste criteria.

### Cited Findings
- Account can be opened from birth until the girl attains 10 years [H] — [Wikipedia: SSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukanya_Samriddhi_Account); [Bank of India](https://bankofindia.bank.in/sukanya-samriddhi-account)
- Max two accounts per family; third permissible where twins/triplets are born (first birth or after one girl) [H] — [India Post SSY eligibility](https://indiapost.org/ssy-eligibility); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/knowledge-center/savings-schemes/eligibility-for-sukanya-samriddhi-yojana)
- Minimum initial deposit ₹250 [H] — [Union Bank of India](https://www.unionbankofindia.bank.in/en/details/sukanya-samridhhi-scheme)
- Interest 8.2% p.a. (rate since 1 Jan 2024, notified quarterly) [M — confirm current quarter] — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/saving-schemes/sukanya-samriddhi-yojana/)
- 4 crore+ accounts opened by Jan 2025 [H] — [News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/over-4-crore-sukanya-samriddhi-accounts-opened-nationwide-govt)

### Inferences
- Max deposit ₹1.5 lakh/yr; deposits for 15 years; matures at 21 yrs; 80C benefit; documents: girl's birth certificate, guardian KYC (Aadhaar, PAN) — background knowledge [M].
- Rule: has daughter aged < 10 AND family has < 2 SSY accounts (or twins exception) → eligible. Resident Indian girl only.

### Gaps
- Official India Post/NSI 2026 quarter interest rate not verified.

## PM Matru Vandana Yojana 2.0 (PMMVY)

### Takeaway
Pregnant/lactating women from disadvantaged categories (any one of SC/ST, 40%+ disability, BPL/NFSA card, PM-JAY, e-Shram, PM-KISAN, MGNREGA job card, net family income < ₹8 lakh, AWW/AWH/ASHA). ₹5,000 for first child (2 instalments) + ₹6,000 for second child only if girl. Age: 18 yr 7 months to 55 yrs at childbirth (not 19). Excludes central/state govt/PSU regular employees or those getting similar maternity benefit.

### Cited Findings
- Age: between 18 years 7 months and 55 years at childbirth [H] — [SPNIWCD (WCD) PMMVY FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)
- Eligible categories (any one): SC/ST; ≥40% disabled; BPL ration card; PM-JAY; e-Shram card; PM-KISAN beneficiary farmer; MGNREGA job card; net family income < ₹8 lakh/yr; pregnant/lactating AWW/AWH/ASHA; NFSA ration card [H] — [SPNIWCD FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)
- Benefit: first child ₹5,000 (₹3,000 after ANC registration; ₹2,000 after birth + 14 weeks immunisation); second child ₹6,000 single instalment only if girl [H] — [SPNIWCD FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)
- Twins/triplets in second pregnancy with at least one girl → eligible for second-girl incentive [M] — [search summary; Niva Bupa](https://www.nivabupa.com/govt-scheme-articles/pmmvy-2-0-explained.html)
- Miscarriage/still birth → treated as fresh beneficiary in future pregnancy [H] — [SPNIWCD FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)
- Excluded: women in regular employment with Central/State Govt or PSUs, or receiving similar maternity benefits under any law [M] — [Delhi WCD PMMVY page](https://wcd.delhi.gov.in/wcd/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojana-pmmvy)
- Documents: Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account, mobile, MCP card, LMP/ANC dates, birth certificate, immunisation details, one eligibility proof [H] — [SPNIWCD FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)
- Telangana and Odisha not implementing (own schemes) [M] — [SPNIWCD FAQs](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs)

### Inferences
- The brief's "age 19+" is outdated/inaccurate — use 18y7m at childbirth. Women receiving statutory maternity benefit (e.g. ESIC maternity benefit) may fall under "similar maternity benefits under any law" — ambiguous for ESIC-covered Neev users; flag and don't auto-qualify [L].

### Gaps
- Official Mission Shakti guideline PDF (wcd.gov.in) not fetched to confirm exclusion wording.

## PM SVANidhi (street vendors) — restructured Aug 2025

### Takeaway
Collateral-free working-capital loans for urban street vendors; lending period extended to 31 Mar 2030; tranches now ₹15,000 / ₹25,000 / ₹50,000; UPI-linked RuPay credit card after repaying second loan; digital cashback up to ₹1,600.

### Cited Findings
- Cabinet (27 Aug 2025) approved restructuring and extension of lending period to 31.03.2030, outlay ₹7,332 crore, target 1.15 crore beneficiaries incl. 50 lakh new [H] — [PIB PRID 2161157](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161157&reg=48&lang=2)
- Tranches: 1st up to ₹15,000 (was ₹10,000); 2nd up to ₹25,000 (was ₹20,000); 3rd ₹50,000 unchanged [H] — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161157&reg=48&lang=2); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/pm-svanidhi-scheme-restructured-extended-to-benefit-11-million-vendors-125082700613_1.html)
- UPI-linked RuPay credit card for those repaying 2nd loan; cashback up to ₹1,600 on digital retail/wholesale transactions [H] — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161157&reg=48&lang=2)

### Inferences
- Eligibility (urban street vendor with Certificate of Vending/ID from ULB, or in ULB survey, or Letter of Recommendation from ULB/TVC); 7% interest subsidy; no income cap — background knowledge from 2020 guidelines [M]; confirm on pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in. Salaried delivery/gig workers are NOT street vendors → exclude unless user self-identifies as vendor.

### Gaps
- Whether restructured scheme extends eligibility beyond statutory towns (e.g. census towns/peri-urban) not verified.

## PM Vishwakarma

### Takeaway
Self-employed artisans/craftspeople in one of 18 family-based trades, age 18+, working in the trade at registration; one member per family; govt employees and their families excluded; no PMEGP/PM SVANidhi/Mudra loan in last 5 years unless fully repaid (Mudra/SVANidhi). Benefits: ₹15,000 toolkit e-voucher, ₹500/day training stipend, collateral-free loans ₹1 lakh + ₹2 lakh at 5%.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum age 18 on registration date; engaged in eligible trade on unorganised/self-employed basis [H] — [pmvishwakarma.gov.in How to Register](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/HowToRegister)
- No loans under PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra in past 5 years [H] — [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/HowToRegister); exception: Mudra and SVANidhi borrowers who have fully repaid may apply; 5 years counted from loan sanction date [M, secondary] — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/business-loan/pm-vishwakarma-yojana/)
- One member per family (husband, wife, unmarried children); person in govt service and their family members ineligible [H] — [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/HowToRegister)
- 18 trades incl. carpenter, boat maker, armourer, blacksmith, hammer & toolkit maker, locksmith, goldsmith, potter, sculptor/stone breaker, cobbler, mason, basket/mat/broom maker, doll & toy maker, barber, garland maker, washerman, tailor, fishing-net maker [H for categories; trade names partly background] — [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/HowToRegister)
- Benefits: ₹15,000 toolkit grant; loans ₹1 lakh (18 months) + ₹2 lakh (30 months), collateral-free, 5% interest to beneficiary; ₹500/day training stipend [H] — [pmvishwakarma.gov.in](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/Home/HowToRegister)

### Inferences
- "Mason" is a construction trade → overlaps with BOCW users. Masons in regular salaried employment (e.g. employer payroll) are arguably not "self-employed" — ambiguous; require self-declaration of self-employment [L].

### Gaps
- Official documents list (Aadhaar, mobile, bank, ration card for family verification) not verified this session.

## National Scholarship – Post-Matric Scholarship for SC, ST, OBC

### Takeaway
Currently ₹2.5 lakh/yr parental/family income ceiling for SC and ST post-matric (Class 11 onward). SC ceiling hike to ₹4.5 lakh proposed by MoSJE but NOT notified as of Sep 2026. OBC central post-matric (PM YASASVI) ceiling reported as ₹2.5 lakh — secondary sources conflict (some cite ₹8 lakh, which is the creamy-layer/other-scheme limit).

### Cited Findings
- SC/ST post-matric income ceiling ₹2.5 lakh/yr; MoSJE plans to raise SC limit to ₹4.5 lakh "from next year" and cap fees — plan disclosed to Parliament standing committee, not yet notified [M] — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/post-matric-scholarship-pms-for-sc-rules-change-msje-course-fee-cap-4-5-lakh-income-limit-next-year-social-justice-and-empowerment); [Careers360 on Parliament panel](https://news.careers360.com/post-matric-scholarship-pms-sc-students-national-fellowship-ugc-parliament-panel-report)
- Cabinet approved ₹59,000 crore PMS-SC scheme (Dec 2020, 60:40 centre-state) [H] — [pmindia.gov.in](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/media-coverage/cabinet-approves-%E2%82%B959000-crore-post-matric-scholarship-scheme-for-4-crore-sc-students/)
- OBC post-matric central cap reported ₹2.5 lakh; another source says OBC/EWS ₹8 lakh — CONFLICT [L] — [IndiaScholarships OBC rules 2026](https://www.indiascholarships.in/guides/obc-postmatric-scholarship-rules-2026); contradicted by [Education for All in India](https://educationforallinindia.com/sc-st-obc-scholarship-schemes-2025-%E2%82%B960000-and-%E2%82%B948000-programs-explained/)
- Eligible: SC/ST/OBC/EBC/DNT students studying Class 11 onward (diploma, UG, PG, PhD) in recognised institutions [M] — [Super Tutor](https://supertutor.in/resources/finance/post-matric-scholarship/)

### Inferences
- Zero-false-positive rule: show as eligible only if student in Class 11+ AND caste certificate SC/ST/OBC AND family income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh. Neev salaried workers earning > ~₹20,800/month alone breach ₹2.5 lakh → most will fail; the scheme applies to their children only if total family income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh.
- Documents (caste certificate, income certificate, previous marksheet, Aadhaar-seeded bank account, fee receipt) — background knowledge [M].

### Gaps
- Official scholarships.gov.in / socialjustice.gov.in guideline PDFs for 2025-26 not fetched; OBC ceiling unresolved; ST scheme (tribal.nic.in) limit assumed ₹2.5 lakh from secondary sources.

## Building & Other Construction Workers (BOCW) welfare boards

### Takeaway
Central BOCW Act 1996 s.12: worker aged 18–59 (has not completed 60) who has done ≥ 90 days of building/construction work in preceding 12 months can register as beneficiary with the State BOCW Welfare Board (fee ≤ ₹50). Benefits are state-designed under s.22 (health/maternity, life/disability cover, education of children, housing, skills, pension etc.).

### Cited Findings
- Eligibility: completed 18 years, not completed 60 years, engaged in building/construction work ≥ 90 days in preceding 12 months [H] — [BOCW Act s.12, Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/78474785/); [AdvocateKhoj bare act](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/buildingandother/12.php)
- Application to Board-authorised officer with prescribed documents and fee not exceeding ₹50; not rejected without hearing [H] — [BOCW Act s.12](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/78474785/)
- Welfare schemes (health & maternity, life & disability cover, skill development, transit housing, education assistance for wards etc.) executed by State/UT boards under s.22 [H] — [search summary citing Act](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/78474785/)

### Inferences
- Benefit amounts vary by state → app can only say "register with your state board". 90-day proof usually = employer/contractor/union/registered-contractor certificate plus Aadhaar, bank, photo — state-specific [M].
- Delivery/warehouse/security workers are NOT construction workers; only show if user's occupation is construction.
