# State welfare schemes for blue-collar and unorganised-worker families: Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Gujarat, Odisha, Punjab, Haryana (current as of Sep 2026)

Research date: 30 Sep 2026. Confidence tags: [H] high (official or several consistent reputable sources), [M] medium (reputable news or several aggregators that agree), [L] low (one aggregator, or sources conflict).
**Big 2026 changes.** New governments took office in **Tamil Nadu** (TVK, CM C. Joseph Vijay, May 2026) and **West Bengal** (BJP, CM Suvendu Adhikari, May 2026). West Bengal has **replaced both** Lakshmir Bhandar and Swasthya Sathi. Haryana's Lado Lakshmi changed in Jan 2026. Punjab's health scheme became universal in Jan 2026. Many official portals (cmchistn.com, myscheme.gov.in, vikaspedia) did not render through the fetch tool, so a lot of detail comes from news sites and aggregators. That is marked below.

## Q1. Which 2 schemes per state are the most used (candidate check)?

### Takeaway
Picks: **Karnataka**: Gruha Lakshmi + Anna Bhagya (Shakti is the most-used by trip count, but its only test is being a woman resident). **Tamil Nadu**: KMUT + CMCHIS. **West Bengal**: Annapurna Bhandar (replaced Lakshmir Bhandar) + AB-PMJAY (replaced Swasthya Sathi). **Gujarat**: PMJAY-MA + Namo Lakshmi. **Odisha**: Subhadra + GJAY. **Punjab**: Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana + Smart Ration Card (Atta-Dal). **Haryana**: Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi + Chirayu Ayushman.

### Cited Findings
- Gruha Lakshmi: ₹76,951 cr paid over 32 installments to 1,24,48,938 beneficiaries as of 21 Aug 2026. The 32nd installment was released 08.08.2026. FY2026-27 budget is ₹28,608 cr [M, aggregator quoting state data] — [KnowledgeKendra](https://knowledgekendra.com/yojana/gruha-lakshmi); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gruha-lakshmi-scheme-karnataka)
- Anna Bhagya covers 1.26 crore BPL families (4.48 crore people) [M] — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/karnataka-cabinet-modifies-anna-bhagya-scheme-will-provide-indira-kit-in-lieu-of-5-kg-rice-3758767)
- WB: about 6 crore Swasthya Sathi beneficiaries are being moved into AB-PMJAY [M] — [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/west-bengal-starts-ayushmaan-bharat?year=2026); [Akashvani](https://newsonair.gov.in/ayushman-bharat-scheme-to-be-launched-in-west-bengal-from-july-2026/)
- Punjab: about 40 lakh families hold smart ration cards (free wheat plus a quarterly "Meri Rasoi" kit) [L, aggregator] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/smart-ration-card-scheme-punjab/)
- Haryana Lado Lakshmi: over 10 lakh women registered since the 25 Sep 2025 launch (as of Jan 2026). A second phase targets about 23.5 lakh women [M] — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/govt-expands-lado-lakshmi-scheme-raises-income-ceiling-to-rs-1-8-lakh/); [HintNews](https://hintnews.com/haryana-lado-lakshmi-yojana-2100-second-phase-1-80-lakh-income-limit-23-50-lakh-women/)
- Odisha Subhadra: 1.88 lakh women added after a WCD and IIM Sambalpur review; about 2 lakh removed after disbursal (for example, found to be ITR filers) [L] — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/subhadra-yojana-2025-application-process-benefits-and-eligibility-criteria); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subhadra_Yojana)

### Inferences
- I dropped Gujarat's Manav Garima, Odisha's KALIA (farmer-only) and TN's construction board as "top 2". For Neev's workers, the health scheme plus the women's cash transfer (or food) are the most-used and most relevant pair in almost every state.
- Construction-worker boards (BOCW) exist in all 7 states, but they need registration with the board. The app could ask "Are you a registered construction worker?" as a separate add-on. Not researched here.

### Gaps
- I did not find a verified beneficiary count for every scheme (for example Namo Lakshmi, PMJAY-MA, GJAY), so the ranking is partly judgement.

## Q2. KARNATAKA

### Takeaway
**Gruha Lakshmi**: ₹2,000/month to the woman named as head of family on the ration card, unless she or her husband pays income tax or files GST. **Anna Bhagya**: automatic for AAY/BPL (PHH) cardholders. Since Oct 2025 it gives 5 kg NFSA rice plus an Indira Food Kit instead of the extra 5 kg rice.

### Cited Findings
**Gruha Lakshmi** (official application via Seva Sindhu)
- Benefit: ₹2,000/month by DBT [H] — [KnowledgeKendra](https://knowledgekendra.com/yojana/gruha-lakshmi); [Karnataka.com](https://www.karnataka.com/govt/gruhalakshmi-scheme/)
- Must be the woman recorded as household head on an AAY, BPL or APL ration card. Married, widowed, divorced or deserted women can apply [M] — [JioInsure](https://jioinsure.in/investment-plans/blog/gruha-lakshmi-scheme-benefits-eligibility-how-to-apply)
- Excluded if the woman **or her husband** pays income tax or files GST returns (announced by CM Siddaramaiah, 2023) [H] — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/gruha-lakshmi-scheme-exclude-income-tax-gst-payees-karnataka-cm-siddaramaiah-178285); [Star of Mysore](https://starofmysore.com/gruha-lakshmi-will-not-smile-on-i-t-paying-women/)
- Women government employees are excluded [M] — [JioInsure](https://jioinsure.in/investment-plans/blog/gruha-lakshmi-scheme-benefits-eligibility-how-to-apply)
- 2026 re-verification: door-to-door survey plus biometric check [L] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/gruha-lakshmi-aadhaar-verification/)
- Documents: ration card (with her as head), Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank account, and husband's Aadhaar (for the tax check) [M] — [Karnataka.com](https://www.karnataka.com/govt/gruhalakshmi-scheme/)
- Source URL: https://sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in (Seva Sindhu portal referenced by [Karnataka.com](https://www.karnataka.com/govt/gruhalakshmi-scheme/))

**Anna Bhagya**
- Eligible: holders of an AAY or BPL/PHH ration card linked to Aadhaar. No separate application [M] — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/anna-bhagya-scheme); [Karnataka.com](https://www.karnataka.com/govt/anna-bhagya-scheme/)
- History: ₹34/kg cash in place of the extra 5 kg (July 2023 to Jan 2025). Rice restarted Feb 2025 [M] — [KnowledgeKendra](https://knowledgekendra.com/yojana/anna-bhagya-scheme)
- **Oct 2025 cabinet decision:** the extra 5 kg rice is replaced by an "Indira Food Kit" (green gram, toor dal, sugar, salt, cooking oil; quantity scales by family size). 5 kg NFSA rice continues [H] — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/karnataka-cabinet-modifies-anna-bhagya-scheme-will-provide-indira-kit-in-lieu-of-5-kg-rice-3758767)
- AAY/BPL cardholders over 75 get doorstep delivery [M] — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bpl-antyodaya-anna-yojana-aay-card-holders-aged-above-75-to-receive-rice-pulses-at-doorstep-3767033)

**Shakti (alternative)**: free travel for women in state non-premium buses. Effectively only needs female + Karnataka resident. Not researched in depth.

### Inferences
- App rule for Gruha Lakshmi: female AND head of family on a Karnataka ration card AND self not an IT/GST filer AND husband not an IT/GST filer AND not a government employee. The questionnaire must ask about the husband's tax status to avoid false positives.
- App rule for Anna Bhagya: has an AAY/BPL (PHH) Karnataka ration card. The ration card type is the whole test.

### Gaps
- No official GO text fetched for Gruha Lakshmi. Whether government pensioners, or women whose husband is a government employee, are excluded is not confirmed [L].
- The Karnataka BPL card criteria themselves (income ₹1.2 lakh/yr, land, vehicle) were not verified here.

## Q3. TAMIL NADU

### Takeaway
**KMUT** (₹1,000/month; TVK promised ₹2,500 but it is "under review") for women heads of family aged 21+ with family income under ₹2.5 lakh/yr. **CMCHIS**: family income ≤ ₹1.2 lakh/yr on a VAO certificate. Cover was raised from ₹5 lakh to **₹25 lakh** per family per year (announced 19 Aug 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (KMUT)**
- Benefit ₹1,000/month by DBT. The new TVK government (formed May 2026) confirmed payouts continue. The manifesto promise of ₹2,500 for women under 60 is "under review" [M] — [Kotak811](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/insights/government-schemes/magalir-urimai-thogai-scheme); [eGovtSchemes](https://www.egovtschemes.com/tamil-nadu-magalir-urimai-thogai/)
- Eligible: women aged 21+, **head of family on the ration card**, permanent TN residents, family income < ₹2.5 lakh/yr. The family must not own a **four-wheeler**, or more than **5 acres wetland / 10 acres dry land** [M] — [Kotak811](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/insights/government-schemes/magalir-urimai-thogai-scheme)
- Excluded: wives of Central/State government employees, bank or PSU staff, income-tax payers, pensioners, professional-tax payers, and elected local-body representatives [M] — [Kotak811](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/insights/government-schemes/magalir-urimai-thogai-scheme)
- Official portal: kmut.tn.gov.in (referenced by [eGovtSchemes](https://www.egovtschemes.com/tamil-nadu-magalir-urimai-thogai/))
- Documents: ration card, Aadhaar, bank passbook (Aadhaar-seeded), and an electricity bill (commonly listed) [L] — [LabhGrow](https://www.labhgrow.in/gov-schemes/kalaignar-magalir-urimai-thogai-eligibility-tamil-nadu)

**CM Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS)**
- Family annual income **< ₹1.2 lakh**, proved by an income certificate from the VAO or Revenue authority. Must be a TN resident whose name is on the family ration card [M] — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/explainer/how-to-apply-for-tamil-nadu-cms-comprehensive-health-insurance-scheme-eligibility-documents-and-process); [Ditto](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/chief-minister-comprehensive-health-insurance-scheme/)
- Also covers migrants from other states living in TN for 6+ months (with a migration certificate) and Sri Lankan Tamil refugees [M] — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/explainer/how-to-apply-for-tamil-nadu-cms-comprehensive-health-insurance-scheme-eligibility-documents-and-process)
- Cover: ₹5 lakh per family per year. **19 Aug 2026:** CM Vijay announced an increase to **₹25 lakh** per family [M, needs GO confirmation] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/chief-minister-comprehensive-health-insurance-scheme-tamil-nadu/)
- Documents: ration card, income certificate (VAO), Aadhaar, and a declaration [M] — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/explainer/how-to-apply-for-tamil-nadu-cms-comprehensive-health-insurance-scheme-eligibility-documents-and-process)
- Official: https://www.cmchistn.com/eligibility (page did not render for me)

### Inferences
- App rule for KMUT: female, 21+, head of family on a TN ration card, family income < ₹2.5L/yr, no car, land under the limits, and she and her husband are not govt/PSU/bank employees, pensioners, or income-tax or professional-tax payers.
- The husband's occupation must be asked. Many blue-collar women whose husbands are PSU contract staff may be ambiguous. Treat them as "not eligible / check".
- The CMCHIS income limit is low (₹1.2L/yr ≈ ₹10,000/month). A worker earning ₹20k/month fails it unless another route applies. The app should ask about family income, not only individual income.
- 2026 changes (₹2,500 KMUT, ₹25 lakh CMCHIS) mean the app should put amounts in config.

### Gaps
- Some aggregators list an electricity limit of 3,600 units/yr and exclusion of women already receiving social-security pensions. I could not verify either [L].
- Whether the TVK government changed KMUT's eligibility (for example an age cap under 60) is unconfirmed.
- The ₹25 lakh CMCHIS GO number and effective date were not found.

## Q4. WEST BENGAL

### Takeaway
After the BJP won in May 2026, **Lakshmir Bhandar was replaced by "Annapurna Bhandar"** (flat ₹3,000/month, women 25–60, effective 1 Jun 2026; existing beneficiaries moved automatically). **Swasthya Sathi was folded into AB-PMJAY** (WB signed the MoU 8 Jun 2026, rollout from Jul 2026, ₹5 lakh/family). Residents outside PMJAY are to be covered by a new "Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana".

### Cited Findings
**Annapurna Bhandar (replaces Lakshmir Bhandar)**
- Approved 11 May 2026, effective 1 Jun 2026, first payment 3 Jun 2026. Flat **₹3,000/month**, with no caste difference [M, insurer blog citing the WCD & Social Welfare Dept] — [SMC Insurance](https://www.smcinsurance.com/government-schemes/articles/lakshmir-bhandar-to-annapurna-bhandar-transition)
- Old Lakshmir Bhandar rates just before the change: ₹1,500 general / ₹1,700 SC-ST [M] — [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/gallery/lifestyle/lakshmir-bhandar-scheme-future-after-2026-wb-polls-annapurna-bhandar-update-8qe7de5); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/india/information/lakshmir-bhandar-form-fill-up-2026-underway-check-last-date-eligibility-and-amount-7322640.html)
- Eligible: women **25–60**, permanent WB resident, Aadhaar-linked bank account in her own name [M] — [SMC Insurance](https://www.smcinsurance.com/government-schemes/articles/lakshmir-bhandar-to-annapurna-bhandar-transition)
- Excluded: income-tax filers, government employees and pensioners, staff of government-aided educational institutions, and people flagged deceased, shifted, deleted or absentee in the electoral-roll revision [M] — same
- Documents: Aadhaar, **voter ID**, residence proof, Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook, photos. Applications 27 May – 25 Aug 2026 at socialsecurity.wb.gov.in or block/municipal offices [M] — same
- Existing Lakshmir Bhandar beneficiaries are migrated automatically [M] — [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/gallery/lifestyle/lakshmir-bhandar-scheme-future-after-2026-wb-polls-annapurna-bhandar-update-8qe7de5)

**AB-PMJAY in WB (replaces Swasthya Sathi)**
- Rollout from July 2026. "Existing beneficiaries under the state health scheme Swasthya Sathi will be incorporated into the Ayushman Bharat scheme" [H, government broadcaster] — [Akashvani / newsonair.gov.in](https://newsonair.gov.in/ayushman-bharat-scheme-to-be-launched-in-west-bengal-from-july-2026/)
- WB signed the PMJAY MoU on 8 Jun 2026 as the 36th State/UT. Cover ₹5 lakh/yr, 1,930 hospitals. A state "Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana" covers residents left outside PMJAY [M] — [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/west-bengal-starts-ayushmaan-bharat?year=2026); [govtschemesindia](https://www.govtschemesindia.com/west-bengal-mukhyamantri-swasthya-bima-yojana/)

### Inferences
- App rule for Annapurna Bhandar: female, age 25–60, WB resident, own Aadhaar-seeded bank account, not an ITR filer, not a govt/aided-institution employee or pensioner, on the electoral roll. No income or caste test was found, which makes it one of the easiest to match.
- Health: since Swasthya Sathi was universal for WB residents and is being migrated, "WB resident" is likely enough for PMJAY or the state top-up. The exact route is unclear, so show it as "likely eligible, verify at CSC", not as a firm match.

### Gaps
- No official GO PDF was found for Annapurna Bhandar. Rules come from an insurer blog that cites the department [M]. Whether women getting other pensions (widow or old-age) are excluded is unknown.
- Eligibility and cover for "Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana" are unclear [L].
- Legacy rule (pre-2026, **labelled old**): Lakshmir Bhandar required Swasthya Sathi enrolment, age 25–60, and no permanent govt job.

## Q5. GUJARAT

### Takeaway
**PMJAY-MA**: ₹10 lakh/family/yr (since July 2023) for PMJAY-eligible families plus MA/MA-Vatsalya families with income up to ₹4 lakh/yr. **Namo Lakshmi**: ₹50,000 over Classes 9–12 for girls whose family income is under ₹6 lakh/yr.

### Cited Findings
**PMJAY-MA (Mukhyamantri Amrutam merged with Ayushman Bharat)**
- Cover raised from ₹5 lakh to **₹10 lakh** per family (revamp launched July 2023) [H] — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/gujarat-govt-launches-revamped-pmjay-ma-scheme-with-rs-10-lakh-health-cover-123071100613_1.html)
- MA Vatsalya: APL families with annual income **up to ₹4,00,000**. Senior citizens from households with income up to ₹6 lakh are also eligible [M] — [Star Health](https://www.starhealth.in/health-insurance/mukhyamantri-amrutum-yojana/); [Bajaj General](https://www.bajajgeneralinsurance.com/health-insurance-plans/pradhan-mantri-jan-arogya-yojana-in-gujarat.html)
- Enrolment is at civic centres or taluka kiosks with biometrics and a family photo. Card distribution started with a PM launch in Oct 2022 [H] — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1868223&reg=48&lang=2)

**Namo Lakshmi Yojana** (Education Dept, launched 2024)
- Girls in Classes 9–12 in government, government-aided or private schools. Family income < **₹6 lakh/yr** [M] — [Vikaspedia](https://schemes.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/schemesall/state-specific-schemes/welfare-schemes-of-gujarat/namo-lakshmi-yojana?lgn=en) (page did not render fully); [govtschemes.in](https://www.govtschemes.in/gujarat-namo-lakshmi-yojana)
- Benefit: ₹10,000 per year in Classes 9 and 10, ₹15,000 per year in Classes 11 and 12, total ₹50,000 (paid partly monthly at ₹500–₹750, plus lump sums on passing) [M] — [schemesinindia](https://schemesinindia.in/schemes/gujarat/namo-laxmi-yojana-gujarat); [aimindia](https://aimindia.in/namo-laxmi-yojana-gujarat/)
- Documents: student's Aadhaar, parent's Aadhaar, bank account, mobile number. Applied through the school [M] — same

### Inferences
- App rule for Namo Lakshmi: has a daughter studying in Class 9–12 in Gujarat AND family income < ₹6 L/yr. The school type is ambiguous (see gaps), so return "check with school" when the school is private.
- App rule for PMJAY-MA: family income ≤ ₹4 L/yr OR holds an NFSA/PMJAY card. That is safe. Above ₹4L, only senior-citizen routes apply.

### Gaps
- School type for Namo Lakshmi conflicts: several sources say "government and grant-in-aid schools", while others include private schools. Also, some say the income limit applies only to private-school students [L]. I could not open the official GR.
- Whether the PMJAY-MA ₹4 lakh MA-Vatsalya limit was revised in 2024–26 is not confirmed.
- Gujarat's own NFSA/food schemes were not researched.

## Q6. ODISHA

### Takeaway
**Subhadra**: ₹10,000/yr (two ₹5,000 installments, 2024-25 to 2028-29) for women aged 21–60 in NFSA/SFSS families or with family income ≤ ₹2.5 lakh. Taxpayers, govt employees and women getting ≥ ₹1,500/month from other schemes are excluded. **GJAY** (formerly BSKY, merged with AB-PMJAY April 2025): all NFSA/SFSS families, ₹5 lakh per family plus ₹5 lakh extra for women.

### Cited Findings
**Subhadra Yojana** (launched 17 Sep 2024)
- Women aged **21–60**, Odisha residents. NFSA/SFSS cardholders are eligible automatically. Otherwise family income must be ≤ ₹2.5 lakh/yr [M] — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/subhadra-yojana-2025-application-process-benefits-and-eligibility-criteria); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subhadra_Yojana)
- Excluded: government employees (self or family), income-tax payers, women getting **≥ ₹1,500/month** (or ≥ ₹18,000/yr) under other government schemes, four-wheeler owners (except tractors and light goods vehicles), and families with over 5 acres irrigated or 10 acres unirrigated land [M] — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/subhadra-yojana-2025-application-process-benefits-and-eligibility-criteria); [thecurrentindia](https://www.thecurrentindia.com/govt/subhadra-yojana-eligibility/)
- Benefit: ₹10,000/yr for 5 years (₹50,000 total), paid in two ₹5,000 installments (around Rakhi and Women's Day) [H — widely reported] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subhadra_Yojana)
- Documents: Aadhaar, ration card (NFSA/SFSS) or income certificate, Aadhaar-seeded bank account in her own name. Official portal subhadra.odisha.gov.in [M] — [edistrictgov](https://edistrictgov.com/subhadra-yojana-odisha/)

**Gopabandhu Jan Arogya Yojana (GJAY)**
- All NFSA and SFSS families in Odisha are eligible, mostly auto-enrolled [M] — [Ditto](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/gopabandhu-jan-arogya-yojana/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gopabandhu_Jan_Arogya_Yojana)
- Cover: ₹5 lakh/family/yr, plus ₹5 lakh extra for women members (₹10 lakh total). Integrated with AB-PMJAY in **April 2025**, so treatment is portable nationwide [M] — [ManipalCigna](https://www.manipalcigna.com/blog/gopabandhu-jan-arogya-yojana); [Zospital](https://zospital.com/blogs/odisha-gopabandhu-jan-arogya-yojana-gjay-guide)
- Official: gjay.odisha.gov.in (per [vishwakarmayojana](https://vishwakarmayojana.co.in/gopabandhu-jan-arogya-yojana/)); [AIIMS Bhubaneswar scheme page](https://aiimsbhubaneswar.nic.in/schemes/gopabandhu-swasthya-kalyan-yojana-bsky/)

### Inferences
- App rule for Subhadra: female, age 21–60, Odisha resident, (NFSA/SFSS card OR family income ≤ ₹2.5L), and not a govt employee, IT payer, 4-wheeler owner, big landholder, or someone getting ≥ ₹1,500/month from another scheme. The "other scheme" question matters: widows on pension may be caught by it.
- App rule for GJAY: has an Odisha NFSA/SFSS ration card. Legacy BSKY also covered all residents in government hospitals regardless of card. Whether that continues under GJAY is unclear.

### Gaps
- Whether "government employee" means self only or any family member varies by source [L].
- Whether the free government-hospital treatment for all residents still applies under GJAY is unconfirmed.

## Q7. PUNJAB

### Takeaway
**Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana (MMSY)**: universal from 22 Jan 2026. Every bona fide Punjab resident gets ₹10 lakh/family/yr cashless, with no income, caste or age limit. You need a Sehat Card (Aadhaar plus voter ID). **Smart Ration Card (Atta-Dal/NFSA)**: roughly 40 lakh families get free wheat. The income limit is disputed (see below), but the exclusion list (taxpayers, >2.5 acres, car/AC, etc.) is well documented.

### Cited Findings
**Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana**
- From 22 Jan 2026 it covers all residents with ₹10 lakh per family per year (upgraded from Sarbat Sehat Bima Yojana). No income, age or caste limit; 824+ hospitals [M] — [SMC Insurance](https://www.smcinsurance.com/government-schemes/articles/mukh-mantri-sehat-yojana-punjab); [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/schemesall/state-specific-schemes/schemes-of-punjab/mukh-mantri-sehat-yojana?lgn=en); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/state-pcs-current-affairs/mukh-mantri-sehat-yojana-in-punjab)
- Documents: Aadhaar plus **voter ID** (proof of Punjab residence). Register at a CSC for the Sehat Card [M] — [pddb.in](https://pddb.in/schemes/punjab-mmsy/); [Zospital](https://zospital.com/blogs/punjab-mukh-mantri-sehat-yojana-guide)

**Smart Ration Card / Atta-Dal (NFSA)**
- Smart cards replaced the old blue Atta-Dal cards from 12 Sep 2020. About 40 lakh families; free wheat plus a quarterly Meri Rasoi kit [L] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/smart-ration-card-scheme-punjab/)
- Exclusion criteria: an income-tax, professional-tax or service-tax payer in the household; over 2.5 acres irrigated or 5 acres barani land; owning a four-wheeler or **AC**; a govt-registered enterprise; **family income > ₹60,000/yr**; a house on a 100+ sq yd plot or a 750+ sq ft flat within MC/council limits [M, reported in Tribune; older rule] — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/in-punjab-single-mothers-hiv-and-cancer-afflicted-to-get-ration-cards-567189)
- Conflict: an aggregator says < ₹3 lakh/yr for the basic card, and cites an ₹8 lakh figure for a "new drive from 22 Jul 2026" [L — likely confused with Pakistan's Punjab "CM Punjab Ration Card"; do not use] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/smart-ration-card-scheme-punjab/)

### Inferences
- App rule for MMSY: Punjab resident with a Punjab voter ID. That is the whole test, and the clearest "yes" in this set.
- Ration card: for zero false positives, apply the exclusion list (no car/AC, ≤ 2.5 acres, no taxpayer, no large urban house) and the strictest income figure (₹60,000/yr). Otherwise say "check at depot". Many Neev salaried workers (₹15–20k/month) will fail the ₹60k test.

### Gaps
- The current (2025–26) official Punjab income limit for NFSA smart cards was not found. ₹60,000 may be outdated [L].
- The MMSY GO number was not found. One source says Sarbat Sehat previously required a smart ration card, J-form farmers, or registered construction/small-trader status (legacy, **labelled old**).

## Q8. HARYANA

### Takeaway
**Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi**: ₹2,100/month (since Jan 2026 amendment: ₹1,100 cash + ₹1,000 into a government RD/FD) for women aged 23+ in a PPP family with verified income ≤ ₹1 lakh and 15 years' Haryana domicile. New categories added in Jan 2026 allow up to ₹1.8 lakh. **Chirayu Ayushman**: PPP-verified income ≤ ₹1.8 lakh is free; ₹1.8–3 lakh pays ₹1,500/yr; ₹5 lakh cover.

### Cited Findings
**Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana (DDLLY)** (launched 25 Sep 2025, gazette notification Sep 2025)
- Age **23+**; annual family income **≤ ₹1.00 lakh**, verified via FIDR (PPP data); applicant, or husband if she married into Haryana, must have lived in **Haryana 15+ years**; ₹2,100 per month per eligible woman; **no limit on women per family** [H, official dept page] — [Social Justice Dept, Haryana](https://socialjusticehry.gov.in/deen-dayal-lado-lakshmi-yojana-ddlly/); [Gazette PDF](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392bbd31f8e0e43a7da8a6295b251725f/uploads/2025/09/20250917408358572.pdf)
- Exclusion: women already getting Old Age Samman Allowance, widow/destitute women assistance, Divyang pension or Ladli allowance (cancer, rare-disease and haemophilia pensions may be combined) [H] — [Social Justice Dept](https://socialjusticehry.gov.in/deen-dayal-lado-lakshmi-yojana-ddlly/)
- Neither she nor family members may be income-tax payers [M] — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/lado-akshmi-yojana)
- **Jan 2026 amendment:** added mothers (income ≤ ₹1.8 lakh) of govt-school children scoring >80% in Class 10/12, of children at NIPUN grade-level competency (Classes 1–4), and of children rehabilitated from SAM/MAM. Payment is now ₹1,100 to the savings account + ₹1,000 into a govt RD/FD (up to 5 years). The Tribune mentions age 23–60 and no eligibility for mothers of more than 3 children [M] — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/govt-expands-lado-lakshmi-scheme-raises-income-ceiling-to-rs-1-8-lakh/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2026/01/01/haryana-cabinet-approves-amendments-in-lado-lakshmi-yojana-expands-its-scope.html)
- Documents: PPP (Family ID), Aadhaar, bank account, domicile proof. Apply via the Lado Lakshmi mobile app [M] — [SarkariYojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/haryana-lado-lakshmi-yojana/)

**Chirayu Ayushman Haryana**
- PPP-verified income **≤ ₹1.80 lakh/yr**: free, ₹5 lakh cover. **₹1.80–3.0 lakh**: ₹1,500 per family per year contribution. PPP is the only mandatory ID [H, official Ayushman Haryana] — [Ayushman Bharat Haryana](https://ayushmanbharat.haryana.gov.in/haryana-ayushman-bharat-implementation-overview/); [Star Health](https://www.starhealth.in/health-insurance/chirayu-ayushman-haryana-health-insurance-scheme/)
- ₹3–6 lakh bracket with a higher contribution: mentioned by one aggregator only [L] — [Star Health](https://www.starhealth.in/health-insurance/chirayu-ayushman-haryana-health-insurance-scheme/)

### Inferences
- App rule for Lado Lakshmi (base): female, age ≥ 23, has PPP, PPP-verified family income ≤ ₹1 lakh/yr, 15+ yrs Haryana domicile (self or husband), no one in family pays income tax, and not on the listed pensions. Treat age 60+ and 3+ children as "check" because the upper limit and child cap may apply only to the new categories.
- Chirayu: PPP income ≤ ₹1.8L gives "eligible (free)"; ₹1.8–3L gives "eligible with ₹1,500/yr". A worker's income on PPP may differ from actual salary, so ask for the PPP income.

### Gaps
- It is unclear whether the 23–60 age band and the "≤3 children" condition apply to the base scheme or only to the Jan 2026 additions. The official page says "23 years or above" with no upper limit [conflict].
- The ₹3–6 lakh Chirayu tier was not confirmed on an official page.

## Q9. Cross-cutting design notes for zero-false-positive questionnaire

### Takeaway
Answering about 10 questions covers almost every rule: state + years of residence, gender, age, family annual income (and state ID income such as PPP), ration card type and whether she is the head, whether she or her husband pays income tax or files GST, whether any family member is a govt/PSU employee or pensioner, car/AC/land ownership, other pensions received, and whether there is a daughter in Class 9–12.

### Cited Findings
- Spouse's tax status is an explicit test in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/gruha-lakshmi-scheme-exclude-income-tax-gst-payees-karnataka-cm-siddaramaiah-178285); [Kotak811](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/insights/government-schemes/magalir-urimai-thogai-scheme)
- Overlap with other pensions is an explicit exclusion in Haryana and Odisha — [Social Justice Dept Haryana](https://socialjusticehry.gov.in/deen-dayal-lado-lakshmi-yojana-ddlly/); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/subhadra-yojana-2025-application-process-benefits-and-eligibility-criteria)

### Inferences
- Six of the 14 schemes changed in 2025–26 (WB ×2, TN ×2 announced, Haryana, Punjab, Karnataka Anna Bhagya). Keep the amounts and limits in a data file, not in code.
- For [L] or conflicting criteria, return "May be eligible — confirm at CSC/office", never a firm "eligible".

### Gaps
- Official GOs were not retrieved for KMUT, CMCHIS, Annapurna Bhandar, MMSY or Namo Lakshmi. A follow-up pass on the official PDFs is recommended before production.
