# State welfare schemes for blue-collar / unorganised worker families — Delhi, Bihar, UP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, MP, Jharkhand (as of Sep 2026)

Research note scope: 2 most-used schemes per state, with eligibility rules precise enough for a zero-false-positive questionnaire. Confidence tags: [H] official source read directly, [M] aggregator/news consistent across 2+ sources, [L] single aggregator or inferred. Research pass was limited (~20 tool calls); several official PDFs/portals could not be parsed (noted in Gaps).

## Delhi — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Delhi Lakshmi Yojana** (the promised "Mahila Samriddhi Yojana", renamed; in force from 1 Aug 2026, ₹2,500/month) and (2) **Delhi BOCW Welfare Board registration** (construction workers). Delhi Lakshmi has the strictest, most machine-checkable rule set of all schemes here — official WCD eligibility PDF read directly.

### Cited Findings
**Delhi Lakshmi Yojana (formerly announced as Mahila Samriddhi Yojana)**
- Renamed from Mahila Samriddhi Yojana to Delhi Lakshmi Yojana [M] — [NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/india/mahila-samriddhi-yojana-renamed-as-delhi-lakshmi-yojana-women-to-get-rs-2500-monthly-check-eligibility-launch-date-248218/); [StudyIQ](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/delhi-lakshmi-yojana/)
- Official eligibility (all must hold) [H] — [Delhi WCD eligibility PDF](https://wcd.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/WCD/marquee-files/delhi_lakshmi_yojana_eligibility_and_documents.pdf):
  - Age 21–60 on date of application.
  - Must be the **eldest eligible woman** member of the family (one per family in practice).
  - Applicant **or her husband or either parent** resident of Delhi for **≥10 years**.
  - Must be a **registered voter of Delhi**.
  - Family unit annual income **≤ ₹2,50,000**.
- Official exclusions [H] (same PDF): woman receiving any Central/State govt pension; **income-tax payee**; **GST filer**; has been a govt servant / holds public office; **more than 3 living children**; family annual electricity consumption **> 2,400 units**; she or any family member is a regular **or contractual** employee of State/Central Govt, PSU, Board, Local Body or any govt organisation, or receives retirement/widow/disability/any other pension or similar govt benefit; she or family member **owns a four-wheeler**; **criminal antecedents**.
- Mandatory documents [H]: Aadhaar; Delhi Voter ID; photo + signature; **recommendation from the constituency MP/MLA**. 10-year residence proof (any one): Voter ID, ration card, driving licence, electricity/gas bill. Age proof: birth cert, school leaving, 10th cert, DL, PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID. — [same PDF](https://wcd.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/WCD/marquee-files/delhi_lakshmi_yojana_eligibility_and_documents.pdf)
- Guidelines in effect **1 Aug 2026**; applications open year-round with monthly cutoff (last day of month); approved applicants paid from following month; ineligible beneficiaries face stoppage **and recovery** [M] — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/ineligible-delhi-lakshmi-yojana-beneficiaries-may-lose-aid-face-recovery-govt); gazette notification page: [Delhi WCD](https://wcd.delhi.gov.in/wcd/gazette-notification-delhi-lakshmi-yojana)
- Benefit split: of ₹2,500, **₹1,500 to a locked deposit (until 31 Jul 2029)** and **₹1,000 to a CBDC (digital rupee) wallet** with spending restrictions [M/L — single news source] — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/ineligible-delhi-lakshmi-yojana-beneficiaries-may-lose-aid-face-recovery-govt)

**Delhi BOCW (Building & Other Construction Workers) Welfare Board**
- Eligibility: age **18–60**, engaged in building/construction work **≥90 days in preceding 12 months** [H] — [Delhi BOCW registration procedure](https://bocw.delhi.gov.in/bocw/procedure-registration-and-renewal-membership)
- Documents: employer/contractor (or authorised trade union for self-employed) certificate of 90 days' work; age proof (Voter ID/ration card/DL); address proof (ration card, passbook, utility bill, Voter ID, DL, PAN, Aadhaar, passport) [M] — [Delhi BOCW](https://bocw.delhi.gov.in/bocw/registration)
- Fee: ₹25 first year (₹20 contribution + ₹5 registration), renewal ₹20/yr [M] — search snippet of [Delhi BOCW](https://bocw.delhi.gov.in/bocw/procedure-registration-and-renewal-membership)
- Benefits (categories): health support, accident compensation, pension, maternity, children's education scholarships [M] — [PSR Compliance (aggregator)](https://www.psrcompliance.com/blog/bocw-registration-delhi)

### Inferences
- Delhi Lakshmi is the natural #1 by reach (targets all low-income women 21–60). The Ladli scheme (girl child) was not verified this pass; BOCW chosen as #2 because it directly matches the blue-collar target user.
- For zero-false-positive: questionnaire must ask (a) eldest eligible woman in family, (b) 10-yr Delhi residence of self/husband/parent, (c) Delhi voter, (d) family income ≤₹2.5L, (e) any family member govt/PSU employee incl. contractual, (f) four-wheeler in family, (g) >3 living children, (h) income-tax / GST filer, (i) any govt pension. Electricity >2,400 units/yr (~200 units/month) is hard for users to self-report — ask "average monthly bill units > 200?" or treat as "likely eligible, verify".
- The **"contractual employee of any govt organisation" exclusion applies to any family member** — many blue-collar workers are contract staff at govt bodies/PSUs (e.g., DMRC, MCD contract sanitation); this is a high false-positive risk and must be asked explicitly.
- BOCW: the 90-day certificate is the real gate; a generic "construction worker?" question is not enough.

### Gaps
- Official definition of "family unit" (does it include adult sons?) not found.
- Whether the MP/MLA recommendation is truly mandatory for all applicants (appears in official PDF) — unusual; confirm.
- Number of Delhi Lakshmi beneficiaries/applications — not found.
- Delhi BOCW specific benefit amounts (2025–26) not verified; board has had suspension/audit issues historically — not checked this pass.
- Delhi Ladli scheme 2025–26 status not verified.

## Bihar — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana** (2025; ₹10,000 first instalment to women via JEEViKA SHGs, up to ₹2 lakh later) — by far the largest by count; (2) **Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana** (cash incentives for girls passing Inter/graduation). Official Bihar documents were not read directly; confidence mostly medium.

### Cited Findings
**Mukhyamantri Mahila Rojgar Yojana (MMRY)**
- Launched by PM on **26 Sep 2025**; ~75 lakh women targeted in first transfer of ₹10,000 each [M] — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/bihar/bihar-assembly-elections-2025-75-lakh-bihar-women-to-receive-rs-10000-each-as-pm-modi-to-launch-mukhyamantri-mahila-rojgar-yojana-3743728)
- A later tranche: ₹2,500 crore released to 25 lakh women [M] — [News On AIR headline](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/bihar-cm-nitish-kumar-releases-%e2%82%b92500-crore-to-25-lakh-women-under-mukhyamantri-mahila-rozgar-yojana) (page could not be loaded; headline only)
- Eligibility: permanent resident of Bihar; age **18–60**; must be a **JEEViKA SHG member** (non-members can join an SHG to become eligible); **one woman per family**; Aadhaar-seeded bank account in own name; declared livelihood activity [M] — [Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/mukhyamantri-mahila-rojgar-yojana/); [eGovtSchemes](https://www.egovtschemes.com/mukhyamantri-mahila-rojgar-yojana/); Vikaspedia entry exists: [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/schemesall/state-specific-schemes/welfare-schemes-of-bihar/mukhyamantri-mahila-rojgar-yojana?lgn=en) (content not loadable)
- Benefit: ₹10,000 first instalment; up to ₹2,00,000 additional after assessment ~6 months later [M] — [Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/mukhyamantri-mahila-rojgar-yojana/)

**Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana**
- Graduation (any stream) completed in window **1 Apr 2021 – 30 Sep 2025**: ₹50,000 one-time [M] — [biharhelp.in](https://biharhelp.in/12th-pass-kanya-utthan-yojana-2025/); [sarkarimint](https://sarkarimint.com/bihar-mukhyamantri-kanya-utthan-yojana/)
- Intermediate (Class 12) unmarried girls: ₹25,000 for 1st division, ₹15,000 for 2nd division [M] — same sources
- Rules reported: permanent resident of Bihar; max two girls per family; excluded if parent is a govt employee [L] — [universityhelper](https://universityhelper.in/mukhyamantri-kanya-utthan-yojana/) (aggregator only)
- Portal: Medhasoft (Bihar Education Dept) [M] — [kanyautthanyojana.info](https://kanyautthanyojana.info/)

### Inferences
- MMRY is effectively gated by SHG membership, not income — the app should route users to "join a JEEViKA SHG" rather than promise eligibility.
- Kanya Utthan is girl-education-linked; relevant to worker families with daughters in Class 12/graduation; eligibility by passing year window changes annually.

### Gaps
- Official MMRY guidelines (JEEViKA/Rural Development Dept) not read: exclusions for income-tax payers or govt-employee families **not confirmed** — do NOT assume none. Unmarried women eligibility rules unclear (some reports say unmarried adult women without parents qualify).
- Whether MMRY applications remain open in 2026 (post-Nov 2025 election) — not confirmed.
- Kanya Utthan: official income limit (reportedly none) and 2026 passing-year window not confirmed; the "parent govt employee" exclusion is from an aggregator and should be verified before use.
- Bihar Laghu Udyami Yojana not assessed (smaller reach; ₹2 lakh for families with income ≤₹6,000/month per memory — unverified).

## Uttar Pradesh — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **UP BOCW Board** (≥1.88 crore registered construction workers; many sub-schemes incl. daughter-marriage aid) and (2) **Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana** (₹25,000 in 6 stages per girl). Kanya Sumangala criteria read from official portal [H].

### Cited Findings
**Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana** [H] — [mksy.up.gov.in](https://mksy.up.gov.in/women_welfare/index.php)
- Family must be resident of UP (proof: ration card, Aadhaar, voter ID, electricity/phone bill).
- Annual family income **≤ ₹3 lakh**.
- Max **two girls** per family; family must have **max two children** total. Exception: third girl eligible if twins in second delivery (or first child girl + twin girls second).
- Adopted orphan girls count toward the two-child limit.
- Stages (total ₹25,000): birth on/after 01/04/2019 ₹5,000; full immunisation (born after 01/04/2018) ₹2,000; Class 1 ₹3,000; Class 6 ₹3,000; Class 9 ₹5,000; enrolment in graduation or ≥2-yr diploma after 10th/12th ₹7,000.
- Increase from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 applied to registrations from 2024–25 onward [M] — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/kanya-sumangala-yojana)

**UP BOCW Board (Uttar Pradesh Bhavan evam Anya Sannirman Karmkar Kalyan Board)**
- Registration: age **18–60**, ≥90 days construction work in last 12 months; ₹20 registration + ₹20 annual contribution [M] — [Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/uttarpradesh/yogi-govt-raises-financial-aid-up-to-rs-85000-for-the-marriage-of-construction-workers-daughters-1020849); scheme list: [upbocw.in](https://upbocw.in/English/StaticPages/schemes.aspx)
- >1.88 crore workers registered [M] — [Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/uttarpradesh/yogi-govt-raises-financial-aid-up-to-rs-85000-for-the-marriage-of-construction-workers-daughters-1020849)
- Kanya Vivah Sahayata (daughter marriage): ₹65,000 normal, ₹75,000 inter-caste, ₹85,000 per couple mass marriage; worker must have ≥1 year (365 days) membership [M] — same source

### Inferences
- Kanya Sumangala's "max two children" is a family-level rule — the questionnaire must ask total number of children, not just daughters, to avoid false positives.
- UP BOCW sub-scheme benefits require continuous renewed membership; ask "registered + renewed for ≥1 year?"

### Gaps
- Kanya Sumangala documents list and whether govt-employee / income-tax families are excluded — not stated on the page read.
- Stage-1 birth cutoff shown on portal as "on/after 01/04/2019" — whether stage 1 has an application deadline (typically within some months of birth) not confirmed.
- UP BOCW official 2025–26 amounts should be verified on upbocw.in scheme pages (only news source read).

## Maharashtra — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana** (₹1,500/month, women 21–65, family income <₹2.5L; heavy e-KYC-based purges in 2025–26) and (2) **MJPJAY + AB-PMJAY integrated** (from 1 Jul 2024: all Maharashtra families covered up to ₹5 lakh/yr). Official GR not parsed this pass; rules consistent across multiple sources.

### Cited Findings
**Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana**
- Women **21–65**, Maharashtra resident; married, widowed, divorced, abandoned, destitute women, plus **one unmarried woman per family**; family annual income **< ₹2.5 lakh** [M] — [SMC Insurance](https://www.smcinsurance.com/government-schemes/articles/ladki-bahin-yojana); [Vikaspedia (Marathi) summary via search](https://socialwelfare.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/92e93993f93293e-935-92c93e932-93593f91593e938/92e93993f93293e90293893e920940-91593e92f926947-935-92f94b91c92893e/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%96%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%9D%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%A3-%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE?lgn=mr)
- Exclusions: any family member **income-tax payer**; four-wheeler registered to a family member (**tractor excepted**); women who are state/central govt employees; receiving benefit under another scheme (≥ the same amount); more than two women per family [M] — [Vikaspedia (Marathi)](https://socialwelfare.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/92e93993f93293e-935-92c93e932-93593f91593e938/92e93993f93293e90293893e920940-91593e92f926947-935-92f94b91c92893e/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%96%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%9D%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%AC%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%A3-%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE?lgn=mr); [SMC Insurance](https://www.smcinsurance.com/government-schemes/articles/ladki-bahin-yojana)
- 2025–26 verification: ~62 lakh removed for not completing mandatory e-KYC; ~16 lakh removed for family income > ₹2.5 lakh (PAN–Aadhaar / income cross-check); e-KYC deadline 30 Apr 2026, now an annual e-KYC window [L/M — aggregators] — [Kotak811](https://www.kotak811.bank.in/insights/savings/e-kyc-for-ladki-bahin-yojana); [sarkariyojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/ladki-bahin-yojana-ekyc/)
- Official portal: [ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in](https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in/dashboard); helpline 181.

**MJPJAY (integrated with AB-PMJAY)**
- From **1 Jul 2024**, integrated scheme covers all families in Maharashtra; cover up to ₹5 lakh/family/yr [M] — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/insurance/mahatma-jyotiba-phule-jan-arogya-yojana); scheme listed on [myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mjpjay) (page not loadable)
- Eligible categories: Yellow, AAY, Annapurna, **Orange** ration cards; **White** ration card holders (incl. govt/semi-govt employees); families **without a ration card but with a Maharashtra domicile certificate**; special groups (ashram school students, orphans, institutional seniors/women, journalists) [M] — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/insurance/mahatma-jyotiba-phule-jan-arogya-yojana)

### Inferences
- Ladki Bahin: the tractor exception matters for rural users — phrase question as "car/jeep/four-wheeler (not tractor)".
- Questionnaire must not claim eligibility on income alone — the 2025–26 purges show the state cross-verifies income tax/PAN data; income-tax filer in family = exclude.
- MJPJAY is effectively universal for Maharashtra domiciles — eligibility question reduces to "Maharashtra resident with ration card or domicile certificate?" (low false-positive risk).

### Gaps
- Official GR (28 Jun 2024 and amendments) not read; documents list (Aadhaar, domicile cert or 15-yr-old ration card/voter ID/birth/school cert, income cert exempt for yellow/orange card holders, bank passbook) is from memory — verify before use.
- Whether ₹2,100/month (manifesto promise) has been implemented as of Sep 2026 — not confirmed; assume ₹1,500.
- Rule for women born outside Maharashtra married to a resident — not verified.
- Whether new Ladki Bahin registrations are currently open — not confirmed.

## Rajasthan — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA) Yojana** (formerly Chiranjeevi; ₹25 lakh health cover via Jan Aadhaar) and (2) **Shri Annapurna Rasoi** (₹8 subsidised meal, no eligibility test) — or, if a targeted cash scheme is preferred, **Lado Protsahan Yojana** (₹1.5 lakh per girl born on/after 1 Aug 2024). Beneficiary counts to rank these were not found.

### Cited Findings
**MAA Yojana**
- Free (state pays premium): NFSA families, SECC-2011 households, small & marginal farmers, contract workers in state depts/PSUs (plus BPL/EWS per some sources); all other Rajasthan residents can enrol for **₹850/family/yr** [M] — [Coverfox](https://www.coverfox.com/government-schemes/health/mukhyamantri-ayushman-arogya-yojana-maay-rajasthan/); [Joinditto](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/mukhyamantri-ayushman-arogya-yojana/)
- Cover: up to **₹25 lakh/family/yr** IPD; ₹10 lakh accident cover reported [M] — [Coverfox](https://www.coverfox.com/government-schemes/health/mukhyamantri-ayushman-arogya-yojana-maay-rajasthan/)
- Mapped to **Jan Aadhaar** family ID (mandatory); infants <1 yr covered even if not added to Jan Aadhaar [M] — [Joinditto](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/mukhyamantri-ayushman-arogya-yojana/)
- Exclusions: non-residents, state/central govt employees & pensioners (covered under RGHS / other govt medical schemes) [L] — [Coverfox](https://www.coverfox.com/government-schemes/health/mukhyamantri-ayushman-arogya-yojana-maay-rajasthan/)
- Official portal: [maayojana.rajasthan.gov.in](https://maayojana.rajasthan.gov.in/#/home)

**Shri Annapurna Rasoi** (renamed from Indira Rasoi, 2024)
- ₹8 per meal, 600 g plate; open to anyone — no eligibility screen [M] — [Govt Schemes India](https://www.govtschemes.in/rajasthan-shri-annapurna-rasoi-yojana); [Wikipedia: Indira Rasoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Rasoi)
- Operator payments pending since Dec 2025 in Dausa (service disruption risk) [M] — [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/dausa-news/rajasthan-shri-annapurna-rasoi-yojana-update-dausa-faces-crisis-over-rs-8-plate-20689226)

**Lado Protsahan Yojana (alternative #2)**
- ₹1.5 lakh in 7 instalments; girl born **on/after 1 Aug 2024** in a govt or JSY-registered hospital, Rajasthan resident; family BPL/SC/ST/OBC or income < ₹2.5 lakh (reported) [L — aggregators only] — [govtschemes.in](https://www.govtschemes.in/rajasthan-lado-protsahan-yojana); [rajasthangovt.com](https://rajasthangovt.com/lado-protsahan-yojana/)

### Inferences
- MAA: for zero false positives the app can safely say "free cover" only if user has NFSA ration card (food security) + Jan Aadhaar; everyone else "eligible with ₹850 premium".
- Annapurna Rasoi has no eligibility logic — useful as a "near you" info card, not a questionnaire result.

### Gaps
- Official MAA guidelines (free-category list, govt employee exclusion) not read; RGHS exclusion is inferred.
- Lado Protsahan official eligibility (esp. whether income ≤₹2.5L or category is the actual rule) unverified — conflicting aggregator phrasing.
- Beneficiary counts for ranking not found.

## Madhya Pradesh — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Mukhyamantri Ladli Bahna Yojana** (₹1,500/month, married women 21–59, rules read from official portal [H]) and (2) **Ladli Laxmi Yojana** (girl child, ~₹1.43 lakh over life cycle). Sambal 2.0 (unorganised workers, labour-registration based) not verified this pass.

### Cited Findings
**Ladli Bahna Yojana** [H] — [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
- Married (incl. widowed, divorced, abandoned) women; age **≥21 as on 1 January of application year and <60**.
- MP permanent resident (sthaniya nivasi); **Samagra ID** (family + individual) required; Aadhaar-linked DBT bank account.
- Excluded if: self-declared family income **> ₹2.5 lakh/yr**; any family member **income-tax payer**; any family member regular/permanent/contract govt employee or pensioner (per portal: govt employment/pension); she already receives **≥ ₹1,250/month** under another govt scheme; family jointly holds **> 5 acres** agricultural land; family owns a **four-wheeler (tractor excepted)**; (portal also lists elected-representative exclusions — not captured in fetch).
- Benefit: **₹1,500/month** (raised from ₹1,250) [H].

**Ladli Laxmi Yojana**
- Parents MP residents and **not income-tax payers**; covers first two girls; family planning required after second child (first-delivery girl eligible without it) [M] — [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/women-and-child-development/child-development-1/girl-child-welfare/state-wise-schemes-for-girl-child-welfare/ladli-laxmi-yojana-of-mp?lgn=en); [Bhopal district](https://bhopal.nic.in/en/scheme/ladli-laxmi-yojna/)
- Total benefit ~₹1,43,000 across schooling stages + final payment at 21 [L] — [mahayojanaa](https://www.mahayojanaa.in/2026/01/ladli-laxmi-yojana-2026.html)

### Inferences
- Ladli Bahna is closed to unmarried women — ask marital status. Also new registrations have been largely closed since 2023 (see gap), so the app should show "check status" rather than "apply" unless a window is open.
- The ≥₹1,250/month other-scheme exclusion matters for widows on pensions.

### Gaps
- Whether Ladli Bahna registration is open in 2026 (enrolment was paused after 2023) — not confirmed.
- Ladli Laxmi exact stage amounts and registration age limit (typically within 1 year of birth) not confirmed from official portal (ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in not fetched).
- Sambal 2.0 eligibility not researched.

## Jharkhand — which 2 schemes, and their exact rules?

### Takeaway
Picks: (1) **Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana** (₹2,500/month, women 18–50, ration card required; official portal read [H]) and (2) Mukhyamantri Sukanya / green ration card — **not verified this pass** (gap).

### Cited Findings
**Maiyan Samman Yojana** [H] — [mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in](https://mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in/)
- Age: completed **18** and **below 50** at application (earlier 21–50; aggregators still show 21) [H for current portal text; conflict noted] — contrast [pw.live](https://www.pw.live/upsc/exams/mukhyamantri-maiya-samman-yojana) (21–50).
- Jharkhand resident; Aadhaar; **single Aadhaar-linked bank account** in own name.
- Family must hold **yellow (Antyodaya), pink (priority household), white, or green** ration card.
- Excluded if: applicant or spouse (father, if unmarried) employed in govt / public sector / govt-aided educational institution or gets pension; family member is/was MP or MLA; **family pays income tax**; applicant has an **EPF account**; already receiving pension from the WCD & Social Security Dept.
- Benefit: **₹2,500/month** (up from ₹1,000 from Dec 2024) [H/M] — portal; [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/jharkhand/jharkhand-women-to-get-rs-2500-under-maiyan-samman-scheme-from-dec-28-3329912)
- ~56 lakh women received first transfer (₹1,415 cr) at launch [M] — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/jharkhand/jharkhand-cm-launches-maiyan-samman-yojana-transfers-rs-1415-cr-to-over-56-lakh-women-3343279)
- From Apr 2025, Aadhaar-linked single bank account mandatory to keep receiving payments [M] — [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/jharkhand/maiyan-samman-yojana-jharkhand-govt-tightens-rule-only-these-women-to-get-benefits-after-march-31-2025-03-26-982340)
- An ₹8 lakh family income cap is reported by aggregators [L] — [policybazaar](https://www.policybazaar.com/life-insurance/child-plans/maiya-samman-yojana/); not on the official portal text read.

### Inferences
- **EPF account exclusion is critical for Neev's users**: formally employed blue-collar workers (e.g., factory, security, delivery with payroll) usually have EPF/UAN — they are **ineligible**. Ask "Do you have a PF/UAN account?" Also, EPF of spouse is not listed — only the applicant's.
- Ration card colour is a hard gate — ask it.

### Gaps
- Second Jharkhand scheme (Mukhyamantri Sukanya Yojana, green ration card / Jharkhand State Food Security Scheme) not researched due to budget.
- Whether the upper age is "<50" or "≤50" in current guidelines; exact treatment of EPF accounts that are dormant — unclear.

## Cross-state notes for the app

### Takeaway
Common exclusion signals across the women's cash transfers (Delhi, Maharashtra, MP, Jharkhand): income-tax payer in family, govt employee/pensioner in family, four-wheeler (tractor excepted in MH/MP), family income cap ₹2.5L (Delhi, MH, MP). Jharkhand uniquely excludes EPF account holders — directly relevant to salaried blue-collar users.

### Cited Findings
- Delhi requires ≤₹2.5L + no family govt/contractual employee + no four-wheeler + ≤3 children — [Delhi WCD PDF](https://wcd.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/WCD/marquee-files/delhi_lakshmi_yojana_eligibility_and_documents.pdf)
- MP excludes >5 acres land and ≥₹1,250/month other benefits — [cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in](https://cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in/)
- Jharkhand excludes applicant EPF account holders — [mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in](https://mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in/)

### Inferences
- Minimum question set covering all 14 picks: state, gender, age, marital status, family annual income band (≤2.5L / 2.5–3L / >3L), anyone in family pays income tax, anyone in family is govt/PSU/contract-govt employee or pensioner, four-wheeler (non-tractor) owned, own EPF/PF account, ration card type/colour, number of children & daughters (+ daughters' ages/class), construction work ≥90 days in last year, JEEViKA SHG member (Bihar), years of residence in state, voter in state (Delhi).
- Where a criterion is [L] or a gap, the app should output "may be eligible — check at official portal" rather than "eligible".

### Gaps
- Beneficiary counts to rigorously rank "most-used" were found only for Maiyan (~56 lakh), MMRY (75 lakh target), UP BOCW (1.88 crore registered), Ladki Bahin (purges of ~78 lakh imply >2 crore base) — not for Ladli Bahna, MAA, Kanya Sumangala, Kanya Utthan.
