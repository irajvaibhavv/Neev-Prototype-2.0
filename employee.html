Terms & Conditions

Salary Advance Facility — Neev NBFC Pvt. Ltd.

1. Nature of Facility

This is a short-term salary advance facility provided by Neev NBFC Pvt. Ltd. (RBI-registered NBFC). The advance is against salary already earned by the borrower and is repaid via employer payroll deduction on the next pay day.

2. Eligibility & Verification

The borrower must be a salaried employee whose employer has an active agreement with Neev. Identity is verified via Aadhaar e-KYC, PAN verification, and employer records (Employee Code Number). Employment and salary are confirmed through the employer's HRMS system.

3. Loan Amount & Tenure

The advance amount is limited to a percentage of the borrower's net earned salary for the current pay cycle. Tenure is from disbursement date to the borrower's next pay day (typically 1–4 weeks). The credit limit may increase based on on-time repayment history.

4. Service Charges & Fees

A service charge of ₹15 per ₹1,000 per week is applicable, plus 18% GST on the service charge. The annualised percentage rate (APR) is disclosed in the Key Fact Statement before every advance. No hidden charges. No foreclosure/prepayment penalty.

5. Repayment & Recovery

The full advance amount (principal + charges + GST) is deducted from the borrower's salary by the employer on pay day and remitted to Neev via Virtual Account Number (VAN). In case of employer delay, E-NACH mandate serves as backup recovery. Late payment attracts continued service charge at the same rate plus any bank bounce charges.

6. Loan Agreement

The loan agreement between the borrower and Neev governs the advance, repayment, and salary deduction arrangement. This is signed electronically via Aadhaar e-Sign.

7. E-NACH Mandate

The borrower authorises an E-NACH (electronic auto-debit) mandate on their bank account as a backup repayment mechanism, activated only if salary deduction fails.

8. Cooling-Off Period

The borrower may cancel the advance within 24 hours of disbursement. In such case, only the principal amount needs to be repaid — no service charge or GST is levied.

9. Data Privacy & Consent

Borrower data (Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements, employment records) is collected and processed solely for credit assessment and loan servicing, in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines (Sept 2022). Data shared via Account Aggregator is encrypted end-to-end per RBI norms.

10. Grievance Redressal

Complaints can be raised via the app or by emailing grievance@neev.in. The Grievance Officer (Aarti Nair) will respond within 30 days. If unresolved, the borrower may escalate to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman (https://cms.rbi.org.in).

11. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in New Delhi.
